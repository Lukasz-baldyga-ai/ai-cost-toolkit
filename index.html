AI Cost Toolkit

Price out an AI build before you start, not after the bill lands.

Two small, honest tools. Neither one is AI under the hood, and both say so.

1. Which model tier do you need?

A free 5-question quiz. Answer a few questions about the job, get back a tier recommendation in plain English. No login, no tracking.

Open the tool

2. AI build cost check (Claude Skill)

An interview-style checklist Claude runs with you before a build: classifies the job, estimates the cost with the math shown, and gives you one control worth applying, not a six-item lecture. Install steps are in the repo.
